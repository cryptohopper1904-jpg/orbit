Rounding it out

The rest of a working day, in the same place.

Beyond the five pillars, Orbit keeps the everyday tools right where the messages already are — so you rarely reach for another tab.

📅Calendar

Your appointments synced in — today and what's next at a glance, beside the mail they came from.

👤Contacts & CRM

Every sender becomes a contact with full history, company and open items.

📚Knowledge base

Facts the AI lifts out of your messages — searchable and linked back to the source.

👁Mail preview

Read and reply in a split view — no signature icons in the way — without leaving the thread list.

🔒Three separate worlds

Private, business and work stay strictly apart. Nothing from one ever leaks into another.

◆ Private by design

Self-hosted on your own server. Your mail, files and contacts never leave it — no third-party cloud reading your inbox.

◆ The AI proposes, you decide

Every reply, forward and booking is a draft. Orbit prepares the work; you review and approve before anything goes out.

◆ One cockpit, many jobs

Messaging, CRM, accounting, document filing, a slide library and a task board — the tools of a work day, in a single place.
